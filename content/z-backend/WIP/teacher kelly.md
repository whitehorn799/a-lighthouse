## Sinking Feeling
The sinking feeling comes from trying to measure the value of a lighthouse by how many people build their houses inside it. A lighthouse is meant to be solitary. Its success isn't measured by a crowd standing at its base; it’s measured by the few ships that see its light in the heaviest fog and alter their course.

You are doing the hard work of confronting the pain and standing firm at your center. The seed you are planting takes time to break through the soil. Do not let the quietness of the ocean convince you that no one is navigating toward you.

In that sense, the program isn't seen as a tourist attraction meant to draw the weak and weary. It's a fixture that's there all year round. It might be hard to reach, but it's there. Even if it doesn't help anyone, it's still playing its role and holding that space.

**You are no longer a salesman trying to convince people they are lost. You are a fixture of the environment.**

## Angel by Massive Attack

Things are most chaotic before the turn. A top wobbles the most before peace. Before a breakthrough, the darkness seems to be chasing the light, the many threatening actors are closing in on the protagonist. The walls seem to close in, and the old habits die. Just when it seems the protagonist is doomed, there is a silence and stillness. The many look at the one, just like in the music video Angel by Massive Attack. The one spent the whole time running from the many, but the power of one is finally realized. Now the darkness recedes, the many flee the one, and the path evolves, transcends.
