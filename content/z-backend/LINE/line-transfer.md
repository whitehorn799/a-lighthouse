## New Theories
1. endowment effect - a cognitive bias where people assign a higher value to things simply because they own them, demanding much more to sell an item than they would be willing to pay to buy it
2. novelty bias - the tendency to give too much importance, attention, or preference to new things just because they are new
3. hedonic adaptation - the psychological process where people quickly return to a stable baseline level of happiness after experiencing major positive or negative life events or changes

## Your Only Support
This is correct. You are your support, but that doesn’t mean you can’t ask for help. You process emotions. They are teachers with a lesson for you. You cannot learn the lesson if you ignore them because they’ll only get louder until you listen. Ask them why you are feeling them, listen to the lesson, accept hard truths, and forgive but don’t forget; otherwise, you’ll repeat the same behavior. Pay attention to your thoughts and behaviors. Notice where you go astray. Stop the patterns.

## People coming in/out of lives
Why do people come into and leave our lives? We have free will, and there is chance. Paths cross for as long as as they need, and forces beyond even choice and chance press upon the folds of time. It’s life, and lamenting even a moment is both inevitable and blasphemous.
