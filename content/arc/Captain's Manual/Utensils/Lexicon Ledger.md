# Part Column
This is the column for the parts of speech.
- CN = a countable noun form (i.e., apples)
- UN = an uncountable noun form (i.e., water)
- V = a verb
- ADJ = an adjective
- ADV = an adverb
- Synonym = a word that means something the same or similar
	- Hard is the same meaning as difficult.
- Antonym = a word that means the opposite
	- Hot is an antonym for cold.

# Term Column
This is the term for the related part of speech. If you see an en-dash (–). there is no part of speech for that word.
