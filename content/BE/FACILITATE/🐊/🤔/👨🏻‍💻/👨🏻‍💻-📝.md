 A person can take the small things for granted, mistakenly forgetting that the small things in life actually matter most. A Learner finishes a project not by doing the minimal effort but after applying their full effort, and doing so with integrity. Importantly, a Learner understands that failing is just what comes before success. It is a necessary part of improvement. The effort to survive and improve might not need to be a struggle, but can be (and most often is) in this reality. Failure is strength. How is this different than the courage to try? This is the monitoring effort of the trying. It's the difference between the captain getting on the ship at all (Courage to Try) and adjusting the rudders to steady the ship's sailing course.
