 A Learner knows the boundaries they must set for themselves to live in dignity. They know what they prefer and what they abhor. They know what keeps their dignity intact and what soils it. A person who possesses self-respect knows how to behave toward themselves, whether it is being honest or maintaining personal values despite how difficult or painful. Respect begins here in everyone. Integrity is doing the right thing when no one is looking; honor is doing the right thing in public.
