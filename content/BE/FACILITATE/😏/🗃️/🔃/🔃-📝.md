This is about the inspiration to start a new cycle of life with the understanding one has gained. It's the idea of releasing the need to feel in control of things other than your choices. The Witness sees everything, and just after the sense of resignation from the dissociative drift comes a realignment. That new alignment is not only letting go of control, but also to be the human you are in the reality you now occupy. There are still some years left, and a Learner understands that one does things not because they have no meaning, but because they are creating their own meaning. After this realignment, one becomes more prepared than ever to do that for themselves.
