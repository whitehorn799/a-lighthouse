It's the knowledge that one's own emotions should never impact others because personal problems are managed by the self. Regardless of fault or blame, a Learner understands that dumping negative emotions on innocent people is never an option. It may be that a person with this quality may still seek guidance with a particularly troublesome emotion. This does not mean that a person should only keep emotions to themselves. Emotions are meant to be expressed, but do so wisely and with courtesy to others.
