This is not only the belief in and doing the morally right thing and that people who really seek help are worth the trouble. It's also _meaning_ it with a full and good heart. People can easily see through faked emotions, and changing oneself to fit situations or other people is a betrayal of the self. People can tell when one is being disingenuous, and it costs nothing to be one's true self. A Learner knows that being sincere is wise because people will give their respect more readily. One should do things because it makes them feel wholesome, not because of how good one looks doing them.
