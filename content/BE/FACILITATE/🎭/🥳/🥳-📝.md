It's important to react to people because we live together. One might be busy, but at least it is fair to respond to someone with kindness when they make an attempt to be social. One may never know if that person they ignored will become important to them later. In a similar sense, it isn't at all fair to release a bad day of emotions on someone else. A person with social-awareness isn't awkward with others or unkind, but they are friendly, kind, and helpful, regardless of their current emotional state.
