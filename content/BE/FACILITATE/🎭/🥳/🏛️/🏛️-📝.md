This is the ability to be an active and good citizen in one's city. This means that one behaves appropriately with kind courtesy and polite manners by following rules and laws. One isn't loud, cleans after oneself and pets, doesn’t litter, uses crosswalks and only when it's safe to walk, takes a caring pride in cleanliness, and more. Trashing the space in which one lives is counterintuitive to a healthy lifestyle. It would be as though one's appearance is well groomed, but a pile trash surrounds their house. A Learner understands that respect is not only for people, but the civic and natural environments in which they live.
