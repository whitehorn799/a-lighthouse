The world is filled with people seeking to press their advantage over others. A Learner helps the innocent when they are weak because of what they feel to be morally right for the sake of one's improvement. They help the guilty when they are overwhelmed by righteousness in excess. For instance, in the face of a bully, a Learner will help the victim. On the other hand, they will protect a known thief against a mob of workers who seek more than a fair punishment.
