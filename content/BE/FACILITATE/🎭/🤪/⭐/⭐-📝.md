A Learner doesn't believe in the idea that they are broken somehow, that they must attach themselves to people or things, that some positive or negative force is affecting them, or that someone else completes them as a person. They are a whole person already, and while there are many blessings that come with a complementary companion, they are not an essential part of the self. This balloons into a broader aspect: faith in humanity. Every person is worth their true effort to improve. A Learner sees people as generally good, trustworthy, and that they don't need saving, just a little guidance. No one is broken; everyone is whole, and they're exactly where they need to be.
