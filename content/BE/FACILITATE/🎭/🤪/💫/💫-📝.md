Everyone has an intuition, which is a gift from one's deepest connection to this reality and all others that (probably) exist. Intuition is the ability to interpret messages from imagination. A Learner understands that imagination is part of intuition; it tells us that other things might be possible in this reality because it thinks outside of it. It dreams up unicorns and dragons, but also unique, practical ideas that may help us in predicaments. First, through imagination, we receive messages that use a personal dictionary of symbols. Next, using emotional feelings, we intuit the meaning of the message. Finally, using reason, we pass the decoded message to our logical mind to figure out the steps of achieving the goal in this reality. Imagination message > Intuition translation > Reason delivery > Logical processing! Intuition is used to guide a person toward their preferences and away from their aversions.
