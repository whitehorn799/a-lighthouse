This goes hand-in-hand with emotional intelligence. A person cannot expect to function well in the world when they have mental crises caused by avoiding painful emotions. There are real mental health problems that may or may not affect a person's ability to be a Learner. For instance, it may be difficult for a person without brain activity to think about their existence. A Learner regularly takes a self-administered diagnostic checkup on their mental health. They process their emotions appropriately. An optimal mental ability can only help a person find their place in reality.
