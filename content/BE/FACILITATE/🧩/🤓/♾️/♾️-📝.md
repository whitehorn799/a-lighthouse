Truth reveals itself in pieces, which lead to a bigger truth. However, the whole truth will never be revealed. It's like [[the captain's parrot]] analogy. We forever get closer and closer to finding the truth, yet it always escapes us. The mathematical concept called an "[asymptote](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/asymptote.html)". It's a line that approaches another line but never touches it because of the infinity between them. A Learner understands how to apply Socrates's "I know nothing" statement and the asymptote concept to life.
