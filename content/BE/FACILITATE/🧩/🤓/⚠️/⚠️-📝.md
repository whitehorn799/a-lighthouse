Sometimes, taking a moment to clarify definitions, phrases, or personal stances is a good practice for clear and open communication. When engaging in discussions with others, it's important to be aware of personal meanings. One way to remember this is the phrase "equal words, equal meaning". Language is clumsy, and we have to filter our huge thoughts through the bottleneck of terminology. This ability is also giving the other person the benefit of the doubt. Instead of reacting emotionally to statements, take the time to clarify their meaning. A Learner understands that this is a preventative measure against misunderstandings.
