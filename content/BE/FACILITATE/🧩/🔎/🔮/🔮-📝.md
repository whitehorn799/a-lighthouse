A person could be so involved in the details of life that they forget they have the ability to effect positive change to their circumstances. If a part of one's life doesn't match their preference, one needn't feel like there's no escape. There's a way to maintain responsibilities while compromising for freedom. It may not be a total release from circumstances that becomes the solution, but it could be. A Learner understands that the clarity needed to change one's circumstances can be crucial to relief.
