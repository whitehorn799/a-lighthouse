1/4 Posting your emotional distress online for public sympathy feels like catharsis, but it could be an addiction.

External comfort gives you a temporary high. This becomes a drug that you hit when you need it. It pushes reward without work.

Here's how to stop the pattern. 🧵


<hr>

2/4 Your ego broadcasts your struggles to the Internet.

It doesn't want a solution to the problem: it wants justification for staying as you are. Every like or comment boosts its validation. 

It swaps instant relief for long-term success.

<hr>

3/4 Genuine desire for change doesn't begin with public sympathy.

It starts with the acceptance of hard truths that many people don't want to speak. Your conscience warns when you ignore your agency.

Sitting in silence long enough makes even the sound of a breath more profound.

<hr>

4/4 Public venting isn't healing. 

Avoid dopamine hits; ask yourself what you're avoiding & why. Move into acceptance; release control.
