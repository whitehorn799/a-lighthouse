1/4 Forcing yourself to focus on positivity is a devious trap.

Optimism that ignores the problems that cause deep, emotional friction is simply avoidance. Also, it's a choice that you make.

Avoiding your emotions and problems comes with a heavy toll. 🧵

<hr>

2/4 You suppress your awareness of what's going on when.

It's a contradiction your brain senses immediately: hiding emotions doesn't resolve them. Instead, it builds anxiety and burnout.

You are just pretending that you have peace in hopes that it will magically appear.

<hr>

3/4 Examine your problems instead of ignoring them.

Peace isn't behind a mask. It's the understanding that growth requires trials. Then, you can ask the hard questions and accept reality without panicking or pretending.

Optimism isn't enough. It's the dark and the light.

<hr>
