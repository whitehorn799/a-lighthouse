You don't suffer from a failure to succeed.

You suffer from the [[Success Trap]], the delusion that reaching the next milestone will finally make you feel complete.

Here is why your mindset is destroying your peace and how to correct it. 🧵

<hr> 

Societies around the world sell us a lie: work hard, achieve the goal, feel fulfilled.

The high from achieving goals only lasts a couple of days. The existential emptiness returns, but now, you're more exhausted than before. Are these goals even yours?

<hr>

The trap lies in being a follower of other people's goals. 

When you refuse to face your internal discomforts, you give your power to external masters. "Success" becomes what other people want from you. It distracts you from asking yourself, "What do I want to do with my life?"

<hr>

Socrates warned against being a follower, yet our modern cultures demand an unquestioned lifestyle.

Personal growth is found in removing blind spots in our behavior. Fulfillment is revealed in intellectual humility. Maybe you are climbing the wrong ladder.
