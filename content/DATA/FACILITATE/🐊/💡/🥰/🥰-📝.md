In the program, self-love means being true to who you are, appreciating your own value, and treating yourself well. You defend what you believe, you define your boundaries, and you don't deny yourself the opportunity to be who you are. While negativity has its place, a positive attitude helps you treat yourself with honest fairness and humble honor. Self-love teaches you to accept painful truths with dignity. It also teaches that you have a significant value, whether it's to the world or to yourself, because you have many things to give. Self-love is accepting your flaws, not thinking you are perfect.
