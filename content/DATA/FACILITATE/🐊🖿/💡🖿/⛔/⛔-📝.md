A person in survival mode certainly knows what they can and can't do. Sometimes, ability can be the thin line between life or death. A Learner knows their limitations but does not begrudge them. They are a necessary tool used to derive meaning from experience. While many limitations are imposed upon everything by the rules of reality, many people unknowingly impose false limitations upon themselves via learned helplessness or conditioning. It can be fun to try to remove some where we can.
