In this program, self-identity means a person's uniqueness, principles, beliefs, agency, self-worth, and narrative continuity. These are known as the strings of our "six-string guitar" metaphor. 
<br><br>
Self-identity is established by asking establishing the ultimate environment of love. Then, we seek to "know thyself" as we ask existential questions and answer them.<br><br>
Everything about a person is irreplaceable. A person has their own body, personality, background, values, and more. They embody a perspective that is entirely their own. Self a way to know oneself so as to proceed by making choices that align with who they are.

**Moral** <br>
You can't love others if you don't love yourself.
