A Learner knows the difference between their personal reality and the shared reality with everything else. What they believe to be real is secondary what is real. They know fantasy from fiction, belief from reality, and opinion from fact. They hold on to their inner reality, walk through the shared reality, and are resilient enough in both to endure damage from both. A Learner is capable of holding on to their inner truths without forcing others to believe in and participate with them.
