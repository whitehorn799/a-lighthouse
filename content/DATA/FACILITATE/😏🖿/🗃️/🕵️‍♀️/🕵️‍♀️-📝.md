 This is the trait that sees morals as objective truths. It means that all actions are valid human experiences that should not be judged as either good or evil, wrong or right. This is because good and evil are illusions and that preference and aversion are the more accurate terms to use. The switch in terminology is a reminder that love accepts all actions, all people, all things because each is an essential, irreplaceable part (at least in this reality). However, there is the overall tilt toward the ultimate good because love pushes for self reflection, responsibility, and improvement. A Learner understands the reason why "evil" and "pain" are "allowed" to happen.
