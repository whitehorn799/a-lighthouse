This is about the realization that chaos comes before calm. Many know the phrase the calm before the storm, but it also works in reverse. It works on different scales: world order, personal life, and weather systems. Both of these phrases exist to explain the ups and downs of natural life. Imagine a toy that spins around on a point. It spins for a long while before it starts to lose momentum. The toy top starts a little wiggle, which turns into a wobble. It's this moment that reveals the slow chaos toward stillness. The calm present before the wiggle returns. When we notice things in our life starting to go "wrong" or chaotic, we know that we will be fine after. Things may be different, but the only thing that never changes is that things change. The Learner understands the idea that all chaos passes, and there's no cause for worry. For the shadows to be cleaned, the light must shine. Lies are exposed before truth can prevail.
