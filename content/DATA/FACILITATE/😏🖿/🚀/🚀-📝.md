After having experienced an awakening, one feels empowered by their discoveries. A person feels a sense of belonging like never before. They feel at home and at peace because they know their life's purpose. This isn't the same kind of motivation that may come with a new year or a new job. The enthusiasm remains rather even. Even the motivation may plateau after it reaches its highest peak. The key aspect is that the motivation never decays. The Learner has been through that many times already, and knowing their purpose is the antidote to motivational apathy.
