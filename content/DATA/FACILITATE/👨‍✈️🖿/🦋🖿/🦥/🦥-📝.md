This trait is about observing one's feelings and delaying the decision to choose a reaction. Reacting instantly could be disastrous, while delaying a response could allow for the issue to be resolved before one chooses a response. This can help to avoid troublesome conflicts and give a little breathing room for stress relief. When a faster response is prompted, one can act upon their logical agility to help. Also, this ability to delay and choose a reaction makes manipulators ineffective. A Learner cannot be pressured into making a choice, and in being asked to do so, senses the potential for a manipulative trap. It's like a captain seeing a waterspout approaching the ship he is sailing. Instead of changing performing any maneuvers, he does nothing, and the waterspout dissipates.
