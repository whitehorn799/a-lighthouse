 This is the trait that allows one to constantly refresh their patience such that it never comes to an end. A Learner is patient enough to play the short and the long game. The short game is all about the day-to-day choices one makes, while the long game extends this idea not only throughout years, but beyond one's current lifetime. The long game extends even to the origin of all things. This is like a captain who meets endless disaster after disaster, yet he takes each day one at a time. Despite all the troubles, the captain's joy for life isn't lost at sea.
