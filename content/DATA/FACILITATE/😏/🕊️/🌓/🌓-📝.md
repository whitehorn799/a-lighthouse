 This is the ability to see through duality as a force in this reality. It's not that duality dissolves, but the reason behind it becomes clear. Nature has extremes, but they exist in order to balance them. The answer for any question is always "yes" because questions may often polarize a topic. Is this right, or is that right? In some cases, one pole of the topic is correct and vice versa. What becomes apparent is that the two opposing extremes are actually the same thing, and one cannot exist without the other. However, the answer to almost every issue is astride the two extremes in order to achieve the balance. Why is the balance so important? It's balance that love calls for because it accepts this and that: all things. We need both to grow in this reality. Thus, there is no evil, no good, no wrong no right; there is only the growth from choice that leads us to our preference.
