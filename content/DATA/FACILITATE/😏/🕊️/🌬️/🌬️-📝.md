In the attempt to observe everything in imagination, one must eventually venture into what happens after death: metaphysics. It may start with simple questions: why are we here on Earth? What is the meaning of life? Is there anything after we die? Many use religion to start answering these questions, evolve into general spirituality, and eventually, they may land on the idea that everything is connected. Everybody is one. This is closer to the ultimate all-inclusive concept of enfolding love.
