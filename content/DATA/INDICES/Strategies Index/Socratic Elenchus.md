>[!invisiblock] 
>An elenchus is a way to cross-examine without judgment. The classical elenchus was designed to break down false logic in a debate to elicit truth. It is the tool used in the dialectic (dialogue) of the [[Socratic Method]] of questioning.<br><br>
>Our Elenchus Elicitation method is slightly different: we don't break you down; we help you inspect where your reality creates friction with the world. It isn't an interrogation or being critical. We are two investigators observing things as they are to discern what's actually going on. <br><br>
