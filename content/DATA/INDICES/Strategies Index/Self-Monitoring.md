>[!invisiblock] **Self-monitor, Self-correct**
>Self-monitoring is where you keep tabs on yourself in all forms, such as mentally and physically. You check to see where your peripheral senses are missing something important. Shift your focus to the problems that have grown too big in the absence of your attention. <br><br>
>Then self-correct by facing those problems down. You don't have to do it without help, but you must be the one to monitor yourself.
