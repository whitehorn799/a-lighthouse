**SMART is an acronym**
- S for Specific: nothing too broad
- M for Measurable: a way to see progress
- A for Achievable: something you can actually do, not what Michelangelo could do
- R for Relevant: let it bring you closer to your dream
- T for Time-bound: give yourself a deadline to finish this goal
