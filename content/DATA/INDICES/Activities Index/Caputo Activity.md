>[!invisiblock] 
>This activity demonstrates the Caputo Effect where your brain alters your senses. Also called the "stranger in the mirror", the effect isn't noticeable at first, but it starts after a few moments. When are the other times when you don't notice that your brain alters your senses?
>
^my-block

# Steps
1. Stand in front of a mirror.
2. Dim the lights.
3. Stare at yourself in the eyes for 5 minutes.
4. Be aware of what happens.

What did you see?

## Explanation
Your brain gets bored looking at the same unchanging image in the dark, so it stops processing it. When it tries to fill the blank spaces back in, it glitches and creates a "stranger" out of the shadows. This explains the distortions that appear in your vision during this activity. This effect is called Troxler Fading.
