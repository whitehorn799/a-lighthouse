>[!invisiblock] 
>Our universe is both vast and tiny, wide and narrow, shallow and deep. You will be thrilled at how big you can be and amazed at how small you are. <br><br>
>When you click the Scale of the Universe 2 by [Cary Huang](https://www.htwins.net/) below, you will see an interactive scale of the universe. At the bottom of the screen, there is a scrollbar. Dragging the circle left goes deeper into the micro (small) universe. You'll see things like rice and a grain of salt. Dragging the circle right goes deeper into the macro (big) universe, where you'll see stars and galaxies. You are encouraged to go left first, then go right.<br><br>
> - [Scale of the Universe 2](https://htwins.net/scale2/)
> 	- How do you feel after seeing that?
