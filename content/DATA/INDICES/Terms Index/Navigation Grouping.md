>[!invisiblock] 
>The Navigation Grouping consists of the step-by-step process your mind goes through to notice what is happening and decide what to do next. These are not physical qualities, but a sequence of actions your mind performs at any given moment in time. It is a part of the The Human Experience ([[Environments]]), more specifically the [[Private Reality]].

^my-block

<br> 

>[!invisiblock|title] Hierarchy
>1. Awareness
>2. Attention
>3. Discernment
>4. Focus
>5. Conscience

| Faculty  | Remark                           | Navigation  | Remark                                                                                                          |
| -------- | -------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Witness  | The ability to neutrally observe | Awareness   | A dimly lit room where you can just make out a few objects.                                                     |
| Instinct | Immediate thought/action         | Attention   | Object A appears  brighter or moves more than other objects.                                                    |
| Emotion  | Interpretation through feelings  | Discernment | You decide how you feel about Object A and determine whether or not you pay attention.                          |
| Agent    | Action in the world              | Focus       | This is like a beam of light, or a flashlight, on Object A, where you pay full attention to it.                 |
| Logic    | Problem-solving cognition        | Conscience  | This is the review of focus or action, and it can come as an after-action report or a risk-management function. |



^my-group
