In this program, consciousness is how you sense, interpret, and learn. It's the sum of all your experiences of existence.

We say that there are three kinds of consciousness. You can think of them existing as a coin.
1. Dissociated Consciousness: the fact-based, emotionless consciousness of the world
2. Self-centered Consciousness: the ego-centric, sensitive consciousness of the self
3. Metaconsciousness: the balance of both sides of the coin
