# 1. Dissociated 
AKA Living Ghost Trap
- Cold facts and pure logic
- High awareness of others
- Disconnected from the self

^living-ghost-trap

>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
>Consciousness that lacks emotion where you view everything as facts
^dissociated-consciousness-def

**Description** <br>
This consciousness describes a person who is emotionally numb. They feel like they are floating above their life, and it's difficult for them to see things from another person's specific perspective. It's hard for them to empathize with people because they are robotic in their thinking. 

The downside to this is that they lose passion, connection, meaning and purpose, and relationship depth. Everything feels like the same boring, shallow experience. To them, people seem like emotional messes that can't think straight. 

<hr>

# 2. Self-Centered 
AKA Main Character Trap
- High drama and emotion
- High awareness of the self
- Disconnected from others

^main-character-trap

>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
>Consciousness where everything is you are the main character, highly emotional, and everyone else is the problem
^selfc-consciousness-def

**Description** <br>
This is when a person believes they are the center of everything because they are trapped inside their immediate emotions, impulses, and defenses. Everything is happening to them, yet nothing is their fault. Every interaction feels intensely personal, urgent, and emotional.

The bad part of this is that they are blind to other people's perspectives. Their immediate reaction is emotional victimhood because their ego is driven by feelings. Their ego simply cannot take a step back to look at facts.

<hr>

# 3. Metaconsciousness
AKA The Measured Observer
- Holds facts and feelings as guides
- High awareness of the self and world

^meta-consciousness

>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
>Consciousness where you see emotions and facts together; the awareness of your consciousness

^meta-consciousness-def

**Description** <br>
Metaconsciousness isn't a real word, but we're using it here, and "meta-" means "beyond" or "above". It's a common prefix used throughout the program.

Metaconsciousness is not a point on the scale between Dissociated and Self-centered, but it is the whole scale. This is when a person can value their personal perspective and the worldly perspective. They can see things subjectively, but resolve problems objectively. 

A metaconscious mind combines both ends of the scale into one, but they are not perfectly balanced in their actions all the time. They experience emotions, are aware of their emotions, and choose to delay decision making until the emotions are processed responsibly. They take the clarity and facts from the Dissociative side while also taking the passion and personal truth from the Self-centered side. However, it doesn't let either side take the lead.

Metaconsciousness should be the goal of a person seeking their own [[Personal Power]]. 

<hr>

# Conclusion
Not everyone fits on the extremes of the scale. It's likely that you are at some point between the two. The idea is to become the scale!

If you are a Self-centered Consciousness, you are driving a car while blindfolded and screaming at the road. If you are a Dissociative Consciousness, you are watching the car from a distance and wondering about the purpose of it. If you are a Metaconsciousness, you are driving the car on a pleasant day, feeling the comfort of driving, and actively reacting to the turns on the road and blind spots in your vision.
