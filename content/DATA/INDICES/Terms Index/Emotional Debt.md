>[!invisiblock] 
>Emotional debt is the buildup of unprocessed emotions or unexpressed needs. This happens when you suppress, ignore, or deny your true feelings instead of understanding, accepting, and integrating them.<br><br>
>The result of emotional debt is a downward spiral of physical and mental health, loss in identity, and a sense of existential disassociation.
