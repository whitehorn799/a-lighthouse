# 1. Subjective View


> [!invisiblock] Subjective View
>The subjective view is how the world looks through your personal experience, as if you are a *subject* being studied. You use all of your senses, but you are not a machine recording raw facts. When you experience an event, your brain unconsciously filters the information based on your belief systems, education, opinions, principles, emotions, and logic. Whether you realize it or not, your brain uses imagination and assumptions to automatically complete missing or unclear information.<br><br>
>For instance, maybe you see a dark object in someone's pocket and assume it's a gun, but actually it's a TV remote control.
> 
> ^subject-view
> 
>Your subjective view is linked to your [[private reality]], where all of your biases are found.
