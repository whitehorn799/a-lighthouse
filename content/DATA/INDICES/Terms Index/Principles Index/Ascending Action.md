>[!invisiblock] 
>>[!faintbox|title] Action changes you, and ambivalence binds you. 
>>Ascending Action means that only activity helps you grow. You refuse to let ambivalence make your choices for you, you don't wait until you feel ready, and you take a small, real-world step. You break the spell of stagnation and build the self-trust you need to grow.
>
>[[Ascending Action]] is the active avoidance of [traps](/tags/trap).You may feel the need to wait until you are ready, your confidence is high, and you're free of doubt. While rest refreshes, you cannot simply think your way into a new you. The time you spend making plans and waiting for the right moment allows complacency to sink in. When you break promises you make to yourself, you teach yourself that your words mean nothing. You lose pieces of your self-trust, and pretty soon, ambivalence to taking action or stagnating takes over.<br><br>
>
