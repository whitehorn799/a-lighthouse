>[!invisiblock] 
>Secondary gain is a term in psychology. By remaining in a state of distress, your brain may experience a comfortable sense of validation, sympathy, and attention. This may help you rationalize excuses to avoid risks, personal responsibility, or even taking action at all. It can build a false sense of comfort in complacency. If comfort in complacency is more important to you than facing the uncomfortable effort of change, you may be subconsciously choosing to keep the status quo.
