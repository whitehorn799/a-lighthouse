>[!invisiblock] 
>If you are waiting to be fixed or saved by someone or something outside of yourself, you may be externalizing your responsibility for yourself. In the [[Stages of Change]], you may be stuck at the Precontemplation level. You might feel like your condition is a mental health condition that the therapist, religious leader, or otherwise needs to fix for you. The trouble is that counseling is based on teamwork. The helper guides you to know how to take action. <br><br>
>It's similar to what we call [[Surrogate Transcendence]].
