>[!invisiblock] 
>This trap happens when you blame external events for your own emotional state, choices, and outcomes rather than accepting accountability for your own actions.<br><br>
>It acts as a shield against guilt or failure, and it creates a [[Secondary Gain Trap|secondary gain]] loop that destroys your personal power in this way: "If I am not the cause of my problems, I am powerless to fix them."<br>

^my-block

<hr>

>[!invisiblock|title] Why "Parachute"?
>When something goes wrong, your ego feels like it is jumping out of an airplane, falling through the air toward a hard landing. You are about to hit the ground of reality and face accountability: _you made a mistake, you messed up, or you need to change._ <br><br>
>To avoid that painful impact, your brain panics and pulls the parachute cord, which acts as a safety net that minimizes the pain. <br><br>
>- _"It wasn't my fault, the instructions were terrible."_
>- _"They started it."_
>- _"I was just tired."_ 
>
>The moment you pull that cord, the parachute opens. It catches the wind, slows your fall, and saves your ego from taking the hit. You get instant relief. You don't have to feel bad about yourself, and you don't have to admit you were wrong.
>

# Symptoms
>[!invisiblock] 
>> [!cleanembed]
> ![[symptom-insert#^my-block]]<br> 
>1. **Victim Logic:** Phrases like "I had no choice," "He made me," or "that's just my luck" cause you to feel like you have no power to save yourself. This leads into the [[Victim Trap]].
>2. **Chronic Blame:** You immediately blame anything or anyone but yourself.
>3. **Defensiveness:** You feel that constructive criticism is an attack against you rather than helpful advice.
>4. **Resentment:** You wait for others to change their behaviors or circumstances first before you think about your responsibility to yourself.
>5. **Passive Complainer:** You allow things to happen to you and then complain about the results. You constantly make critical remarks, but you seldom change behaviors. In psychology, this is [[Secondary Gain Trap]].
>6. **Savior/Fixer Complex:** You are waiting to be fixed or saved by someone or something outside of yourself, whether that's a therapist, a relative, a friend, or a god. This is similar to what we call [[Surrogate Transcendence]].
>
>^surrogate-trans-context-1
>

# Plan of Attack

>[!invisiblock|title] Focus on
>1. Avoiding decisions while emotional and calming yourself.
>2. Noticing the pause between your surge in emotions and the next action you take.
>3. Taking responsibility for mistakes.
>4. Understanding that mistakes are natural in the [[Love as the Ultimate Environment|the ultimate environment of love]] and that they are required steps for true personal growth.
>5. Watching your thoughts and noticing the harm in actions you take.
>

^focus-on

>[!invisiblock|title] Micro Actions
>1. **Language Audit:** Use [[Self-Monitoring]] to listen to what words you think or say. Actively change phrases like "They made me" to "I choose to", or "I have no choice" to "I can choose."
>2. **[[The Pause]]:** Take a pause for a few minutes (or even a few seconds) before you react. During this, write down one specific action you can take right now to address your current issue responsibly.
>3. **Daily Micro:** Set one tiny, easy promise to yourself every morning, like "I will do one sit-up" or "I will make my bed." Doing the promise helps rebuild the trust you have for yourself, your inner trust. You actually prove that your actions are dictated by your inner world, not the external world.
