> [!invisiblock]
> This is the inability to relax due to a driving sense to be productive. You can't slow down to enjoy a movie, read a book, or enjoy silence in the slow passage of time. You are always trying to optimize your ability to make something. As a result, it's difficult to spend time in still thought to process emotions from the day.
