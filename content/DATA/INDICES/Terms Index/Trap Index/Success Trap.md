>[!invisiblock] 
>When you find yourself having achieved the dream of a successful life, you may not feel fulfilled. You may discover that you never wanted any of what society calls success: a university education, a corporate job, a family, a house, children, a car. Maybe you have been chasing someone else's idea of a dream, and somewhere along the way, you discarded your own dream to adopt theirs. Welcome to the success trap. 

^my-block

>[!invisiblock|title] My Symptoms
>1. Feeling unfulfilled by having achieved what society labels as a successful life
>2. Feeling [[Existential Drift]]
>3. Not understanding who you are
>4. Breaking promises to yourself

^my-symptoms



<hr>

# Plan of Attack
>[!invisiblock] 
>1. Go inward to look for your own answers.
>2. Explore your [[Inner Environment]] through [[Self-identity]] and find what your desires are
>3. Use [[SMART Goals]] to make your goals attainable

^my-block
