>[!invisiblock] 
> If you feel that you are always the victim of something no matter how minor, you may have adopted the idea of being the "unfixable broken one". You may be avoiding accountability for past decisions, experiences, or circumstances, and you may be exhibiting patterns of [[Limiting Belief Trap]], like learned helplessness or conditioned behavior. You may feel like nothing can be done to avoid your problems or that it's simply fate or bad luck. This mindset makes it easier to stay in therapy, receive sympathy and care, and constantly complain without ever having to risk failure from attempts at real growth.
