> [!invisiblock]
>One of the biggest traps of all is encountered in so many situations. Your emotions are great tools, but if they're ignored, they're incredible obstacles. <br><br>
>Suppose you have a deep desire to accomplish a goal. If you are strong in your agency and your identity, you may understand the work and sacrifice that come with accomplishing that desire.<br><br> 
>If you are in a more vulnerable state, your subconscious may be yelling for you to avoid the [[Desire#^desire-pains|desire pains]]. As a result, maybe you decide to go the path of least resistance and conserve your energy. Both are actions that numb can your system of desire totally, especially with repeated suppression. 
> >[!captionator|center]
> >![[emotional-avoidance-trap.png]]
> >*A Lighthouse* 
> >
> You tell yourself that the struggle to achieve the desire to achieve the goal isn't worth the effort. Like a baby asking for a toy, your desires keep wanting to be realized. However, like a protective parent, your subconscious mind keeps telling your desires to sit down and be quiet. As a result, you unwittingly convinced yourself you want nothing.
