>[!invisiblock] 
>The ability to see things from different viewpoints is called changing your [[Frame of Reference]]. Your point of view is unique, but it exists alongside a vast pool of other unique perspectives. In addition, human awareness, sensation, and knowledge is very limited. <br><br>
>By seeing something from the senses of another person, you become that person in a way. It's almost as if you are watching a movie starring a talented actor, and you become part of their character. You adopt their problems and feel their emotions as your own. This is called [[empathy]], and it is a vital tool for helping us live with others. <br><br>
