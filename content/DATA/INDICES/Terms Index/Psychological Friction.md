>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
> The mental anguish felt when your expectations do not match what you get in reality
>
^my-def

<hr>

>[!invisiblock] 
>Psychological friction is defined as the emotional backlash that results from mismatched expectations and reality. It's the result of the struggle between your private reality and the shared reality. It's characterized by emotional responses, and it's solved by logical reasoning.<br><br>
