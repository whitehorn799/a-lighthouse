This is how you can find what you want in life.

>[!announote|tac]
>Where am I? <br> + <br> Who am I? <br> = <br> What do I want?


1. **WHERE** gives you a direction and a motivation.
	- What does my family need?
	- Who depends on me?
	- Which relationships align with me and which don't?
	- What features of my external world don't match my inner world, and how can I change that, even if my contribution seems tiny?
	- I accept that I am a human right now, so how does that reconcile with how annoying I find human needs?
2. (plus) **WHO** shows you the nature of the light or dark you have to share with the external world.
	- What are the values, principles, and beliefs in my inner world?
	- What kind of person do I want to be?
	- Does my background story control my future, or can I fix past traumas to have a better future?
	- If I don't work to see my inner world in the external world, how can I better the world and reach my dream?
	- What circumstances and which people have issues that only my unique perspective can guide?
3. (equals) **WHAT** hints at what you want.
	- If I can achieve this desire without the external world—without anyone knowing, without any praise, and without any validation—would I still want to do it?
	- If the answer to step 3 is yes, then this is something that aligns with you. If it's no, then it means that you don't actually want it. You only want people to see you get it.
