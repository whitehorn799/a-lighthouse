# 1. Private Reality
AKA Inner Environment, Inner World, Mind's Eye
- Subjectivity: personal meaning 
- Personal experience: attachments, expectations
- Contains inner environment

This represents what is true in your own mind.

It is where consciousness and existence combine to create an experience based on emotions, logic, and instinct. Each consciousness has a specific perspective and a unique experience.

AKA Outer Environment, Outer World, Real World

- Group experience: limitations, actions, consequences
- Contains outer environments

# 3. Dimensional Reality
AKA Cosmic Background
- Subjectivity and Objectivity: acceptance, adaptation
- Sensing the personal experience while observing the group experience
- Contains inner and outer environments

This is container for all realities, and it's the ability to accept and adapt to the actions, limitations, and consequences of the real world and the attachments, expectations, and meaning of the inner world. 

**Dimensional Literacy** is the understanding of how psychological friction between mismatched private and shared realities is a critical and inevitable part of true growth. 

^dimensional-literacy-def

It's identifying and empathizing with a person while also seeing the larger perspective of fact. The merger of the two represents a consciousness that is connected to a higher awareness of available information and relatable experiences.

# Conclusion

Dimensional Reality is not about forcing the world to match your mind, nor is it about allowing the world to erase your identity. The purpose of dimensional literacy is to stop fighting the stressors of reality. 

Instead, adapt to the psychological friction that naturally occurs at the border between the private and shared reality. Accept that friction is inevitable, and take ownership of the consequences of your choices. This is the natural state of your relationship with reality. Friction is physics, not punishment.

You are present in all the realities mentioned here because you have a consciousness, and you exist. The scale is not a choice of which team you are on, but a gradient of experiences. Where you land on this scale simply shows how deeply you understand the interaction with yourself, others, and the world.
