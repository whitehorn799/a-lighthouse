This is creative rest: the passive, nonjudgmental reception of enjoyment. This is the opposite of the creative state, which is necessary to equate a balance to prevent burn-out.



>[!invisiblock|title] Passive Enjoyer
>True creative sovereignty means having a secure internal boundary. It's so secure that you can completely step off the conductor’s podium, sit in the grass, and just listen to the music of the room without needing to tune it or control it. You can step out of the office, leave the blueprints behind, and enjoy a shallow, simple, or beautifully passive moment of fun without guilt.

^passive-enjoyer
