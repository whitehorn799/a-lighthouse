> [!invisiblock] 
>A desire is a deep-seated want associated with you; it's not a casual impulse. It's when the self seeks to express its inner world in the external world. It's your identity assessing your immediate, external environment and deciding how it wants to contribute.<br><br>
>Many people think that wanting something is a fun exercise, but actually, it is full of pain because there are usually challenges to overcome.<br><br>
>When you declare a deep desire, the three Desire Pains become immediately clear to your subconscious mind. 

^definition

<br>

> [!invisiblock|title] Desire Pains
> With every desire comes an alert of what it takes to accomplish it.<br> 
> <div class="color-point cp1 tal">Threat of Failure</div>  
>You might try and fail, then be forced to face your weaknesses.<br> <br>
><div class="color-point cp1 tal">Gap Pain</div>  
> You feel the acute sting of having to work to close the skill or effort gap.<br> <br>
><div class="color-point cp1 tal">Threat of Vulnerability</div> 
> You admit to yourself and the world that you care enough about something to risk being ridiculed, which may break your heart. 

^desire-pains
