>[!invisiblock] 
>[[Elenchus Elicitation]] is a directive bridge between two methods of counsel: Motivation Interviewing (MI) and the [[Socratic Elenchus]]. <br><br>
>In step one of this method, we use MI's empathetic approach (pathos) to support and validate the desires that stem from your [[private reality]]. We compare what you want or expect to what you are actually getting from the world ([[shared reality]]) currently. We elicit the pros of what feels good about your desires, and then we elicit the cons about what the pros cost you. We then clearly define the [[friction]] between the private and shared realities. It reveals a [[Ambivalence State|state of ambivalence]] where you may start to see unhealthy [[Secondary Gain Trap|secondary gain]] patterns and other [traps](/tags/trap). <br><br>
>In step two, we use the elenchus (logos) to investigate how the desires of your private reality compare to the rigid rules of shared reality. We learn that to begin a compromise, we must manage expectations with [[Logic]]. We sense that the world doesn't react to willpower alone. There are physical laws in place, and while we may not agree with them, we must follow them.<br><br>
>Finally, we come to the Elenchus Elicitation method, which bridges pathos and logos. In step three, we learn to reconcile the differences between the desires of one's private reality and the actuality of our shared reality. You learn how to live with what you want and what you get. By identifying the specific traits of your personal power that need nurturing through achievable goals, you may bring yourself closer to a harmonic inner peace that helps you expand your perspectives with emotional and logical understanding. <br><br>
