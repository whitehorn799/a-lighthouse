Imagine a fish swimming happily in a creek and a monkey sitting calmly in a tree above. The monkey notices the fish, and immediately rushes down the tree and into the creek.

The monkey says, "Kindly let me help you, or you will drown". 

The monkey scoops up the fish and puts it "safely" in the tree.
