This is a tale where mother nature is giving gifts to all the animals except the human. 

>[!invisiblock] 
> Mother Nature gave the animals their gifts. To the mole, she gave the gift of insight. To the eagle, she gave perception, and to the whale, she gave patience. <br><br>
> The time came to give the human its gift, but she struggled. She thought that she ought to hide it somewhere the human would not find it easily. <br><br>
> The eagle said to hide it at the top of the mountain, but she said that the human will go there one day. The whale said to hide it at the bottom of the ocean, but there again she said the human will go. The mole said to hide it inside them. Mother Nature agreed, for it was the one place the human would avoid.
