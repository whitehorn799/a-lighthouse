# Collectors
These are pages that collect similar materials, like inspirational quotes, vocabulary, or other important stuff. It's a reference page, or index, that shows all the material. Here is an example of what you will find on the [[Quotes]] page.

# Footnotes
A footnote is a small note at the bottom of a page, and every footnote has a footnote reference (a small number in the top right corner of a word). Sometimes, you will see a footnote like this:

(Click ⬇ the footnote reference, that small number 1.)

Be sure to click those to see what the extra information says!
