### Unit 5: The Agent


This is the mandatory first lesson. It gives the student a starting point, especially in the captain's theme metaphor. The goal is to show the student that they are very small in the scope of life, what they know is very limited, and they can only control what they choose to do.  It teaches them that they should not trust everything, everyone, and sometimes, even themselves. They should try to keep an open-mind, learn what they can trust, and learn how to survive.

**The Theme Perspective** (what is happening in the theme while in this lesson)
The captain-in-training opens his eyes and finds out that he is on his boat. The sails are furled, and there is a stillness in the air. Clouds block the sky, and a fog blocks the ocean. He can only see a few meters. Time seems to stand still. The captain can't remember what happened, where he is, or what he was doing. How do you think he feels?

## Captain's Brief
This is like the moment when you first open your eyes as a baby, but now, you can see and understand more clearly. You are taking this moment to look around you and notice the things in your life. 

Where are you? Where are you going? Who is around you? Who are the ones who care? Who are you?
