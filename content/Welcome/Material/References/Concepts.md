**Boundary:** 
a rule that says how others may treat you
^boundary

**Cognitive Dissonance:** 
the physical discomfort a person feels when new information doesn't match their private reality ^cog-diss

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; margin:1.5em 0 !important; padding: 0 !important; opacity: 25%;">───────♡───────</div>

[[Self-Love#^dignity|Dignity]]
the ability to respect oneself honestly, fairly, and openly ^dignity

**Domino Effect:** 
a causal event which causes other predictable events to happen as an effect ^domino

[[Perspective#^empathy|Empathy]]
the ability to sense other people's feelings with compassion ^empathy

**Equilibrium:** 
a person's sense of the stability and order of the shared reality ^equil

[[Self-Love#^grace|Grace]]
the ability to handle difficulty with thoughtful leniency and sincerity ^grace

[[Self-Love#^humility|Humility]]
the ability accept compliments without feeling better than other people ^humility

[[Self-Love#^peeve|Pet Peeve]]
an act, behavior, or situation that irritates you ^peeve

[[Self-Love#^principle|Principle]]
a boundary that you set for yourself ^principle

**Private Reality:**
every belief, opinion, and practice that is true for you ^private-reality

**Shared Reality:**
the real world in which everyone and everything lives ^shared-reality
