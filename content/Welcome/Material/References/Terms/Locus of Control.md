# Locus of Control
> [!info|invisiblock] 
> This is how a person seeks self-validation from the external or internal world. It's called the locus of "control" because it gives control over your mental state to the internal world (you) or the external worlds (other people and events).
> 
> It relates to the [[Glossary#^relative-absolute|Relative and Absolute Self]] and [[Realistic Confidence]].
> ^locus-control
