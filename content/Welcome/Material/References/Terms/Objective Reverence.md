# Objective Reverence
> [!info|invisiblock] 
> This is the attempt to examine the events, objects, feelings, of our lives with a nonjudgmental, matter-of-fact, and observational vantage of respect and honor.
> 
> objective: ensures there is no emotional distortion, projection, or desperate grasping for approval
> 
> reverence: ensures that you are treating the component with the high respect it deserves as a living piece of creation
> ^object-rev
