# Love as the Ultimate Environment
> [!info|invisiblock] 
> This is the biggest environment because everything exists within it—good and evil. It penetrates and creates everything, like the Force from Star Wars. It prefers unity over division. Behaviors may be misaligned with love's preference of unity, yet love is the all-accepting environment because it does not reject, judge, or abandon.
> 
> Love appreciates you; it understands who you are. It accepts you because love is inclusive. It exists even for individuals who cause harm because it is their chance for redemption. Without love, there would be no hope. 
> 
> You needn't earn love because you already have it. You will never be separated from love because it accepts you. Since unity is inclusive, the environment of love prefers progress toward unity rather than progress toward division. Love wants what is best for you, and those who prefer evil deeds may need them to grow. It may seem that you are removed from love, yet you are not. You may be blinded because of traumas; it may seem buried because of abuses, but it is waiting for you to remove the blindfold and dig it up. 
> 
> Since you cannot be separated from love, the mistakes you make are not mistakes, but exactly what you need to progress. ^love-environ
