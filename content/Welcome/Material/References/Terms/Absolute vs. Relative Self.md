# Absolute vs. Relative Self
AKA separate vs connected self

This is a chart to describe the difference between internal and external validations. These are illustrated on the [[Self-Identity Cross]] as the vertical axis.

> [!info|invisiblock] 
><br>
><div class="redpoint">Absolute Self</div>
>
>It's absolute because it is independent of the external world. This is characterized by a mutual respect for others, a noticeable inner peace, and a strong connection to life as a whole. Validation is intrinsic and comes from the deep connections you have with your goals, environment, your self-identity, life purpose, and much more.
>
><br>
><div class="redpoint">Relative Self</div>
>
>It's relative because of how it relies on its relationships with the external world. The relative self (or base self) features the instinctual or relative self: anxieties, fears, impulsiveness, defense mechanisms, chemical cravings, emotional avoidance, negative loops, and self-centered vantage. 
>
><br>
><div class="redpoint">Struggle For Balance</div>
>
>Your struggle between the relative and absolute self driven by your preference between unity and individualism, your thoughts and actions, and the consequences of them. The balance depends on the story of your life (narrative continuity), especially in your principles, values, beliefs, the strength of your intuition, and the manner in which you process emotions and make logical decisions.
>^relative-absolute-block

**The Absolute-Relative Self Chart**

| Feature              | Relative Self                               | Absolute Self                                       | 
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Question** | "Where do I stand?"                         | "What is true?"                                     |
| **Source of Worth**  | External validation and competition         | Internal alignment and existence                    |
| **State of Mind**    | Anxiety, scarcity, and separation           | Peace, abundance, and unity                         |
| **Perspective**      | Looking _at_ the world from a localized ego | Looking _with_ the world as an interconnected whole |
