# Learning Points
These are the focus of each power you have. They are questions that are important for you to both ask and answer. These are the guiding questions that will lead your thinking and frame your growth to reclaim your personal power. 

These may take a lot of time to answer, or they may be fast. However, if these questions aren't at least asked, we may suffer from unforeseen emotions. Be sure to glimpse at Misery Points to get an idea of what may happen if you don't ask such questions.
