>[!invisiblock] 
>The ability to see things from different viewpoints is called changing your [[frame of reference]]. Your point of view is unique, but it exists alongside a vast pool of other unique perspectives. In addition, human awareness, sensation, and knowledge is very limited. Did you know that humans see only [0.0035%](https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/visible-light-reading-rainbow-nnsas-missions) of the spectrum of light in the universe? If this is true, and our other senses are similarly limited, it would be amazingly arrogant for anyone to know that they know anything. <br><br>
>By seeing something from the eyes of another person, you become that person in a way. It's almost as if you are watching a movie starring a talented actor, and you become part of their character. You adopt their problems and feel their emotions as your own. This is called [[empathy]], and it is a vital tool for helping us live with others. <br><br>
