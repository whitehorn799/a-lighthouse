This is the process through which we can find out what we should work on first. No matter what it is, we have to focus on one issue at a time. We cannot be distracted away from it until it is understood and a goal is planned.

We will identify issues specific to your life. Then, I will look at the abilities of personal power and [[Cognitive Distortions]] to determine how your issues work within the program.

We can use the [[Eisenhower Matrix]] to determine what is the most important. Then, you can choose where to start the dialectic process. 

We can also use [[Positive Coping]] behaviors to work through difficult emotions.

# Interview Questions
When answering these, try not to remain on the surface level. Try to be honest with yourself and provide the real answer, not excuses or cover-ups. 

**Locate the Exhaustion**
The objective here is to find where you are spending your energy fighting a losing battle.

- What is the specific 'current' you feel like you are constantly swimming against?
- What is the immediate consequence you are most terrified will happen?
- Where in your life are you struggling to maintain control?

**Locate the Friction**
The goal here is to find blind spots, avoided questions, and where you might be growing negatively.

- How do you usually react when things go against your expectations?
- What are three important issues that you may be ignoring?
- What are the three most important things you would change about yourself?

**Pin the Blockage**
The goal here is to filter the conversation into the abilities of personal power.

- Think of three patterns, situations, or recurrences that leave you feeling the most powerless, isolated, or drained.
- For each of these, what have you tried to resolve them?
- Which one of these should we start with?

# Cycle
Once we work through a problem we focused on, we go to a new problem. We continue the cycle until you decide to stop.
