# Validation
> [!info|invisiblock] 
> In the context of human existence, this is the need for you to be seen, heard, and acknowledged as a valuable person. It reassures you that your internal world and private reality (your thoughts, feelings, and existence) matters and is sensible. It gives a sense of belonging to the world and in your human body. 
> ^validation

Your [[Locus of Control|locus of control]] can help you see how you seek validation.
