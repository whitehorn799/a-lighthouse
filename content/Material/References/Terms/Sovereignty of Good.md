Love cherishes both good and evil, but it is driven by intention, preference, and growth toward unity.

Love moves to accept, support, and prioritize, while evil rejects, isolates, and victimizes. Progress and growth are the preferred path to idleness, but growth isn't exclusive to positivity. We can grow negatively, too. 

The aim is to respect everyone's preferences, safety, and well-being while coexisting together.
