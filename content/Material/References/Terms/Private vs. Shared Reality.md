# Private vs. Shared Reality
These represent your inner reality and the external environment.

> [!info|invisiblock] 
> <br>
> <div class="redpoint">Private Reality</div> 
>
> what is true in the subjective view of an individual; changed through consequences and willpower; invisible to other people
> 
> <br>
> <div class="redpoint">Shared Reality</div>
> 
> what's true in the objective view of the real world; changed by action
