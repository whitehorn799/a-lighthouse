>[!invisiblock|title] Summary
> This is the world you inhabit together with other people, objects, and places—from vast galaxies to small atoms. It is the outer landscape of physical facts, places, and events that multiple observers can experience.<br><br>
>This is shaped by time, space, and physics, and it is constantly changed by the [[Feedback Loop]]. <br><br> 
> The shared reality is both the classroom and the teacher, but it is neutral to your success: it neither favors nor despises anyone. However, the systems behind it retain nearly full control of what happens to you physically and, in some cases, mentally. 

^my-block

# Components

**Environments**<br>
These are the spaces in which everything outside of your mind exists. [[Environments]] include all dimensions whether or not human beings can detect them.

**Psychological Friction**<br>
Generated by the difference between your expectations and what actually happens, [[Psychological Friction]] is the struggle that naturally happens between the private and shared realities.
