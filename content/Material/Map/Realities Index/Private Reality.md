# Components

**Personal Power Traits**<br>
These are the 100+ traits that this program outlines. The [[Personal Power]] traits are those that a highly efficient person uses to make decisions.

**Echo of Elsewhere** <br>
This is the feeling that you forgot something about a different reality, as if there is a big hole in your spirit. Not everyone feels the [[Anamnesis]] to the same degree.

**Faculty** <br>
This is a toolset of natural abilities that come with being a human in the world. You use the [[Faculty]] to experience life and your environment. These will help you find your personal power.


**Picnic Baskets** <br>
These tools are called "[[Behavior Sets]]" (or simply "baskets") because each group cherry picks specific personal power traits, just like a picnic basket would have a variety of foods.

For example, the [[Defense Basket]] holds all the tools needed to protect you, such as rationalization, lying, and deflections. 


**Inner Observatory** <br>
This is how you make observations. The [[Vantages]] includes the Observation Deck (or Subjective Consciousness) and the Telescope (Objective Consciousness). Since subjectivity is a lower skill than objectivity, everyone starts at the first step of this tool.

**Psychological Friction**<br>
Generated by the difference between your expectations and what actually happens, [[Psychological Friction]] is the struggle that naturally happens between the private and shared realities.
