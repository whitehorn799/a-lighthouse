>[!invisiblock|title] Summary
>This inner world is shaped by your mind, which is the collection of your thoughts and emotions. It changes through willpower, experience, and education, and it can be influenced by your physical body and environment. <br><br>
> It's a private world only you can sense, and it's used to help you interact with and understand yourself. It helps you interpret the outer world so that you can survive it. <br><br>
>Think of the inner world as a chaotic dream. While logic is like a detective peering in and trying to make sense of it all, it can't feel it. The logic interpreting the chaos can structure the dream by organizing it, but it could also be flawed and totally wrong. Therefore, you can use logic to detect patterns, but don't entirely disconnect from emotions to do so.

# Components

**Personal Power Traits**<br>
These are the 100+ traits that this program outlines. The [[Personal Power]] traits are those that a highly efficient person uses to make decisions.

**Echo of Elsewhere** <br>
This is the feeling that you forgot something about a different reality, as if there is a big hole in your spirit. Not everyone feels the [[Echo of Elsewhere]] to the same degree.

**Faculty** <br>
This is a toolset of natural abilities that come with being a human in the world. You use the [[Faculty]] to experience life and your environment. These will help you find your personal power.


**Picnic Baskets** <br>
These tools are called "[[Picnic Baskets]]" (or simply "baskets") because each group cherry picks specific personal power traits, just like a picnic basket would have a variety of foods.

For example, the [[Defense Basket]] holds all the tools needed to protect you, such as rationalization, lying, and deflections. 


**Inner Observatory** <br>
This is how you make observations. The [[Inner Observatory]] includes the Observation Deck (or Subjective Consciousness) and the Telescope (Objective Consciousness). Since subjectivity is a lower skill than objectivity, everyone starts at the first step of this tool.

**Psychological Friction**<br>
Generated by the difference between your expectations and what actually happens, [[Psychological Friction]] is the struggle that naturally happens between the private and shared realities.



>1. [[Navigation Grouping]]: This is what gives you direction. It's also part of your agency. It guides you through your attention, focus, preference, and conscience.
>2. **Self-respect:** This is the foundation. It gives stability through principles, beliefs, self-personal boundaries, and narrative continuity through your self-identity, from the past up to now.
>3. **Self-expression:** This is the inner world moving outward. It gives forward movement through your uniqueness, self-worth, and agency.
