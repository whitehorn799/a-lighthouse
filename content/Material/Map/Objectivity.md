# Components


>[!invisiblock|title] Objective View
>The objective view is how the world looks through factual evidence. It looks at things as *objects* by removing biases, emotions, and interpretations. It's every observable fact, whether or not humans are aware such facts exist. Unlike the subjective view, this is the machine that is recording facts! It is only concerned with what is actually happening. Because it is emotionless, it doesn't mean it's not useful. The objective view allows you to zoom out of a situation and examine it from a nonjudgmental vantage point.<br><br>
>By stepping into this neutral position, you can pause the action and "zoom around" between everyone's subjective views. In the program, zooming around is called changing your [[frame of reference]], and it gives you the space to make reasonable guesses about what they might be experiencing internally. This is a powerful doorway to [[empathy]].<br><br>
>One important detail about objectivity is that it depends heavily on the senses and what humans can observe. If we can't sense important information, we are blind to it. Did you know that humans see only [0.0035%](https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/visible-light-reading-rainbow-nnsas-missions) of the spectrum of light in the universe? If this is true, and our other senses are similarly limited, it would be amazingly arrogant for anyone to claim to know anything for certain.
>
>^object-view
