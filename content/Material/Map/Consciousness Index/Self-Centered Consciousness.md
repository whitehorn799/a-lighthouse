>[!invisiblock|title] Summary
>This is when a person believes they are the center of everything because they are trapped inside their immediate emotions, impulses, and defenses. Everything is happening to them, yet nothing is their fault. Every interaction feels intensely personal, urgent, and emotional. <br><br>
>The bad part of this is that they are blind to other people's perspectives. Their immediate reaction is emotional victimhood because their ego is driven by feelings. Their ego simply cannot take a step back to look at facts.
