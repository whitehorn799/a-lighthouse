# Components

**Dissociated Consciousness**<br>
This defines when you cannot identify with the subjective world. With [[Dissociated Consciousness]], you see things from logical facts more than you have emotional feelings. This is also called the Living Ghost Trap because it feels like you are drifting through a world you don't belong to anymore.

**Self-Centered Consciousness**<br>
This is when you are too focused on yourself to care about anyone else. In [[Self-Centered Consciousness]], your emotions drive you, nothing is your fault, and you are surrounded by morons. This is called the Main Character Trap because it seems like everything is based on you.

**Metaconsciousness**<br>
This is when you have a good balance between logic and emotion. In [[Metaconsciousness]], you hold on to facts just as well as you consider your feelings, and you shift which to use in any situation. This is called the Measured Observer because you take the time to see what's happening, determine how to approach it, and provide a solution.


<hr>

# Conclusion
Not everyone fits on the extremes of the scale. It's likely that you are at some point between the two. The idea is to become the scale!

If you are a Self-centered Consciousness, you are driving a car while blindfolded and screaming at the road. If you are a Dissociative Consciousness, you are watching the car from a distance and wondering about the purpose of it. If you are a Metaconsciousness, you are driving the car on a pleasant day, feeling the comfort of driving, and actively reacting to the turns on the road and blind spots in your vision.
