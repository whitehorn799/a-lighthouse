>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
>1. Existence that unites mental clarity with physical wellness
>		- The harmony of facts and emotions
>2. Consideration of life beyond death
>		- The acceptance and comfort of existence after death
^my-def

<br>

>[!invisiblock|title] Also Known As
>- True Experience
^my-aka

<br>

>[!invisiblock|title] Summary
>In meaning 1, it describes the near perfect unification of mind and body. You are able to process the psychological friction of the differences in your private world and the real world with relative ease. <br><br>
>In meaning 2, it's about the thought of existence beyond body and mind. You think beyond your current life and into the afterlife and come to a comfortable solution, even if you aren't sure about it.
^my-block

<br>

>[!invisiblock|title] Description
>Meaning 2 is the result of facing death as not only an inevitability, but as a mystery that you have already personally addressed. Even though you may not be absolutely sure what happens after you die, you are completely comfortable your beliefs regarding life after death.<br><br>
>This begins to take on a spiritual tone, but the purpose here is not to say that a particular spiritual or religious practice is correct. It's simply the idea that you have come to terms with death, and beyond that, you have a feeling that you exist in a new, nonphysical body when the physical one fails.<br><br>
>Science hasn't proven this yet, but humans don't experience all of reality, either. Keeping this view is a healthy practice in being open-minded to what reality is like. Keep the humility of the knowledge that lies beyond the attempts of our best minds here on Earth to prove life after death.
