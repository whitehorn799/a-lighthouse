>[!invisiblock|title] Definition
> Your automatic, body-based intelligence that monitors your physical state and external environment for immediate survival purposes
>
^my-def

>[!invisiblock] 
>Instinct continuously analyzes your body and the world around you to detect patterns, gauge safety, protect you, assess your baseline healthiness, generate gut feelings, and "vibe check" your surroundings. <br><br>
>It functions as your bodyguard and your body's manager in the real world. It doesn't use words, and it doesn't need your permission or focus. Its only job is to keep you safe long before any logic or emotions come into play.
